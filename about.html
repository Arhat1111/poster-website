WHY WE STARTED.

College rooms are temporary, but they do not have to feel generic. InkWave began around the idea that students should be able to build a space around what they actually like—music, cinema, travel, motorsport, art or their own memories—without expensive décor.

MADE FOR PERSONAL SPACES.

Every InkWave product is designed around accessible pricing, flexible sizes and a simple custom-build process. The store is being built by college founders from NITK Surathkal, with the goal of making wall art easier to discover, personalize and order across India.
