What are the poster sizes and prices?+

A6 ₹19, A5 ₹39, A4 ₹69 and A3 ₹99. The size offers are applied automatically in the cart.

Can I upload my own design?+

Yes. Select “Design your own,” upload a JPG, PNG or WebP file, then choose your size and finish.

What paper do you use?+

InkWave posters are presented as 300 GSM quality prints with matte lamination as the standard finish.

How are payments processed?+

Checkout uses Razorpay for supported UPI, cards and other enabled payment methods.
